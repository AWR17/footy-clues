The Scouting Report

The Scouting Report is a daily football guessing game — one Premier League player a day, revealed through five real clues from their career, earliest first.

How it works

Every day, a new player is drawn from everyone who's made at least 100 Premier League appearances across at least three different clubs since the competition began in 1992. Their career is broken into "stints" — spells at each club — and five of those stints become your clues: the year, the level of football, how long they were there, and their goals and appearances. The club name itself stays hidden until the final clue.

Guess as early as you like. The earlier you get it, the more points you score — but you're never forced to wait for more clues before guessing.

The data

Every clue is built from real statistics, not invented or approximated. Career data is sourced from API-Football and covers the full history of the Premier League, from the 1992/93 season to today. A new puzzle is generated automatically each morning.

Why some legends never appear

The eligibility rule (100+ Premier League appearances across 3+ clubs) is deliberate: it's what makes a five-clue "career path" puzzle work in the first place. It also means a one-club legend — someone who spent their whole career at a single team — won't show up in the main game, however famous they are. That's a known trade-off of this format, not an oversight.

Who built it

The Scouting Report is an independent, hobby-built side project made by a football fan who wanted a daily football puzzle with a bit more depth than a set of initials. There's no company, no login, and no tracking beyond what's needed to remember your own progress on your own device.
