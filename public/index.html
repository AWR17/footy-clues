How to play

1. One player, five clues.

Each clue is a real stint from the player's career — the year, league level, time there, goals, and appearances. The club name stays hidden until the final clue.

2. Guess whenever you like.

You don't have to wait for all five clues. Type a name and hit Guess after any clue — you're not forced to reveal more first.

3. Earlier is worth more.

Clue 1 is worth 100 points, dropping to 80, 60, 40, then 20 on the final clue. The fewer clues you need, the higher your score.

4. Hints cost points.

Stuck? Reveal a hint (nationality + position) for −15 points off your eventual score. It doesn't use up a clue.

5. Limited guesses.

You get 8 guesses per day. Wrong guesses don't cost points, but running out ends the puzzle.

6. Come back daily.

One new player every day. Solve on consecutive days to build a streak — miss a day and it resets.
