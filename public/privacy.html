The Scouting Report is built to need as little of your data as possible. This page explains exactly what is and isn't collected.

No account, no login

There's nothing to sign up for. Nobody — including whoever runs this site — can see your individual results, guesses, or identity.

What's stored on your device

Your game history (which days you've solved, your scores, your current streak) and preferences (light/dark theme, club-reveal mode) are saved using your browser's local storage, a standard feature that keeps small amounts of data on your own device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device automatically
  • Is not visible to this site's operator
  • Is only shared elsewhere if you deliberately copy a "backup code" (from the stats view) and paste it into another device yourself, to bring your history across
  • Can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this page

Third-party services this site uses

Each day's puzzle content is generated using data from API-Football, a football statistics provider. This happens automatically behind the scenes and involves no data about you or your visit — it's purely how the puzzle content itself gets built.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts' servers, which, like most font or content delivery networks, may log the request (including your IP address) as a normal part of serving the file.

Advertising and cookies

This site does not currently show ads. If advertising (such as Google AdSense) is enabled in future, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, which can include using data about your visits to this and other sites. Where required (including for visitors in the UK and EEA), you'll be asked for consent before any such cookies are set. You can review or change how Google personalises ads to you at any time at adssettings.google.com, and find broader industry opt-out tools at youronlinechoices.eu.

Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Because there's no account system and no data is collected beyond what's described above, there's no personal information to request the removal of — clearing your browser's local storage removes everything this site has stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated from time to time, for example if advertising is switched on or a new feature changes what's stored. Check back here if you want the current version.
