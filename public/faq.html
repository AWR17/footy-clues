What is The Scouting Report?

A daily guessing game: one real Premier League player, five real clues from their career, revealed earliest-first. Guess the player from as few clues as you can.

How do I play?

Read the first clue, then either take a guess or reveal the next one. You can guess after any clue — you're never forced to reveal more first. The club name only appears on the final clue (or earlier, if you switch on "Clubs: Shown" mode).

How is my score calculated?

Clue 1 is worth 100 points, dropping to 80, 60, 40, then 20 on the final clue — so the fewer clues you need, the higher your score. Using the paid hint costs 15 points off whatever you eventually score.

What are hints, and do they cost anything?

There are two kinds. A paid hint (nationality + position) costs 15 points and is optional — reveal it any time. There's also a free hint: after three wrong guesses, the player's nationality is shown automatically at no cost.

How many guesses do I get?

Eight per day. Wrong guesses don't cost you any points, but running out ends that day's puzzle.

How does the daily streak work?

Solve the puzzle on consecutive calendar days and your streak climbs by one each day. Miss a day without solving it, and the streak resets the next time you visit.

Why don't some famous one-club players ever appear?

The player pool only includes those with 100+ Premier League appearances across at least three different clubs — that's what makes a five-stint career path possible. A player who spent their whole career at one club won't be eligible for that reason, however well-known they are.

Is my progress saved? Do I need an account?

There's no account and no login. Your results, streak, and settings are saved only in your own browser's local storage on that device — nothing is sent to a server.

Can I play on more than one device?

Each device keeps its own separate history by default. If you play on your phone and a laptop, you can bring one device's history to the other using a backup code — open the stats view (the chart icon in the header) and use "Copy backup code" on one device, then "Import backup code" on the other. It merges your progress rather than overwriting it.

How often is there a new puzzle?

Once a day, published automatically in the early hours (UK time). If you ever see the same player two days running, the daily update hasn't landed yet — check back later.

I think a clue or career path looks wrong — what do I do?

It happens occasionally with any data-driven game pulling from a third-party football database. There's no contact form yet, but feedback is always welcome — see the Privacy page for how to get in touch.

Does this site use ads or cookies?

See the Privacy page for the full detail — in short, no account and no tracking beyond your own device by default, and if ads are ever enabled, you'll be asked for consent first where required.
